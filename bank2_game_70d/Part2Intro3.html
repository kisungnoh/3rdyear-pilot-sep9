Earnings
This part lasts for {{ part2_rounds }} games. At the end of today's experiment, the computer will randomly select three games for payment.

  • Selected game #1: you will earn points based on the decision you make, exactly like in Part 1.
  • Selected game #2: you will earn either 0 or 4 points, based on your assessment about the other's Period 1 decision.
  • Selected game #3: you will earn either 0 or 4 points, based on your assessment about the state of Your Bank.

Note: Your Period 2 assessment is unpaid and used for research purposes only.

The payment rules for the two assessment tasks are designed purposefully by economists to maximize the chance of winning the prize when you submit what you truly think. If you'd like to see the technical details on the payment rule, you may click here.

Payment rule - Assessment about the Other Participant's Period 1 Decision

The computer will randomly draw two numbers. Each number is between 0 and 100, and all numbers in this range are equally likely to be drawn. The two draws are independent, meaning that the outcome of one draw does not influence the other. To determine your payment, the computer will compare these two numbers with the percentages you submitted using the bar.

  • If the other participant withdraws in Period 1: you will be rewarded if the percentage you submitted for the participant's withdrawal is larger than at least one of the two draws.
  • If the other participant stays in Period 1: you will be rewarded if the percentage you submitted for the participant staying is larger than at least one of the two draws.

If this rule sounds confusing, simply remember:

Submitting your assessment truthfully will maximize your chance of winning the prize.
Payment rule - Assessment about Your Bank's State

The computer will randomly draw two numbers. Each number is between 0 and 100, and all numbers in this range are equally likely to be drawn. The two draws are independent, meaning that the outcome of one draw does not influence the other. To determine your payment, the computer will first randomly pick one of the three states: Low, Medium, or High. Suppose the computer picks the Low state, and you submitted 29% as the likelihood of Low, which means 71% for NOT Low (i.e., Medium or High).

  • If the actual state was Low: you will get rewarded if the percentage you submitted (29%) for the Low state is larger than either one of the two draws.
  • If the actual state was Medium or High: you will get rewarded if the number you submitted for your bank NOT in the Low state (71%) is larger than either one of the two draws.

The same logic applies when the computer picks your report about the Medium or High state instead.

If this rule sounds confusing, simply remember:

Submitting your assessment truthfully will maximize your chance of winning the prize.

Your earnings from the three selected games will then be displayed and converted to dollars at a rate of 1 point = {{C.REAL_WORLD_CURRENCY_PER_POINT}} dollar.
You do not know which games will be selected for payment, so it is in your best interest to treat every game as if it is the one selected.
