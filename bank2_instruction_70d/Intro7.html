Earnings

After both you and the other participant finish decisions, you will see the other participant's decision and the points you earned. This concludes the game.

You will play 10 games in this part. At the end of the experiment, one game will be randomly selected for payment, and your earnings from that game will be converted to dollars at the rate of 1 point = {{C.REAL_WORLD_CURRENCY_PER_POINT}} dollar.


Pairings

At the start of each game, you will be randomly paired with another participant. That means you are likely to play with a different participant in each game.
