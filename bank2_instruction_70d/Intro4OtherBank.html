Since you don't know the bank state, you will be given three pieces of information to help you make your decisions:
1) Other Bank withdrawals, 2) how the bank state is determined, and 3) the recommendation.

1) Other Bank Withdrawals

Recently, groups of people like you participated in an experiment at Indiana University. The experiment ("recent experiment”) was exactly the same as the one described so far, except that: participants knew their bank's state before making their decisions. While you will see three payoff tables without knowing the state, they were shown only one payoff table that matched their actual bank state. Click here to view a sample screen from the recent experiment.

In each game you play today, one game from the recent experiment will be randomly selected. We will refer to the bank in that game as the Other Bank. The bank in which you play as a customer today will be called Your Bank.

When you make decisions (withdraw vs. stay), the Other Bank withdrawals will be displayed, which shows the final number of participants in the Other Bank who chose to withdraw in either Period 1 or 2 (W or SW).

  • Since each bank had two customers, the Other Bank withdrawals can range from 0 to 2.
  • A new Other Bank will be selected for each game you play, so this number may vary from game to game.
