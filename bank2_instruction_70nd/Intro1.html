Overview

In this part, you will be randomly paired with another participant and play 10 games. In each game, both you and the other participant will act as customers in the same bank.

  • You start each game with {{C.ENDOWMENT}} points in your deposit account.
  • You will decide whether to withdraw your money or stay with the bank.
  • The bank can be in one of three states: Low, Medium, or High.

Depending on the bank state and the other participant's decision, your same decision may lead to different payoffs.
