Decisions

In each game, you will make decisions over two periods.

In Period 1, you and the other participant in your bank simultaneously choose whether to withdraw or stay.

  • If you withdraw, you immediately take out your 10 points (entire deposits) and make no more decision in this game.
  • If you stay, your deposit remains in the bank and you advance to Period 2.
