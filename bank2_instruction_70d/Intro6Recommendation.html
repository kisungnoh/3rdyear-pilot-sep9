3) Recommendation

If you choose Stay in Period 1, you will receive a recommendation from a computerized agent when making decision in Period 2. The agent observes Your Bank's state and the other participant's choice in Period 1, and sends a message according to the rule below:

Recommendation When you receive it
Withdraw
  • The state of Your Bank is Low, or
  • The state of Your Bank is Medium, and the other participant chose Withdraw in Period 1
Stay
  • The state of Your Bank is High, or
  • The state of Your Bank is Medium, and the other participant chose Stay in Period 1

The recommendation does not reveal which specific case applies. However, the recommendation is always truthful.


Please note:

  • The recommendation arrives in Period 2. You will not receive a recommendation if you choose Withdraw in Period 1.

These three pieces of information, the Other Bank withdrawals, how the bank state is determined, and the recommendation, may help you have a better idea about Your Bank's state and make better decisions (withdraw, stay) in Period 1 and 2.
