The summary below will be shown as a Reminder during the game.

Summary
  1. You will start a game with a randomly paired participant, who is likely to change from game to game.
  2. As a customer at a bank, you will decide whether to withdraw your money or stay with the bank across two periods:
    • W: Withdraw (Period 1)
    • SW: Stay (Period 1) → Withdraw (Period 2)
    • SS: Stay (Period 1) → Stay (Period 2)
  3. Your payoffs depend on Your Bank's state, your decision, and the decision of the other participant you are paired with.
  4. In today's experiment, the state of either the Other Bank or Your Bank is unknown. Therefore, you will need to make decisions (withdraw or stay) in each period under the uncertain state.
  5. To help you make decisions, you will see the Other Bank withdrawals, which show the final number of customers at the Other Bank who withdrew in either Period 1 or 2 (W or SW).
    • Participants at the Other Bank knew their bank's state exactly when making decisions.
  6. The state of the Other Bank was randomly determined with the following probabilities:
    • Low with 15% probability
    • Medium with 70% probability
    • High with 15% probability
    The state of Your Bank is determined as follows:
    • With 70% probability, Your Bank is in the same state as the Other Bank.
    • With 15% probability each, Your Bank is in one of the other two states.
  7. If you stay in Period 1, you will receive a recommendation from a computerized agent when making decision in Period 2.
    Recommendation When you receive it
    Withdraw
    • The state of Your Bank is Low, or
    • The state of Your Bank is Medium, and the other participant chose Withdraw in Period 1
    Stay
    • The state of Your Bank is High, or
    • The state of Your Bank is Medium, and the other participant chose Stay in Period 1
  8. You will play {{ C.PART1_ROUNDS }} games in Part 1. At the end of the experiment, one game from this part is randomly selected for payment (1 point = {{C.REAL_WORLD_CURRENCY_PER_POINT}} dollar).
