The table shows the payoffs that you and the other participant can earn when the bank state is Low.
The labels (W, SW, SS) represent the possible decisions:

  • W: Withdraw (Period 1)
  • SW: Stay (Period 1) → Withdraw (Period 2)
  • SS: Stay (Period 1) → Stay (Period 2)

The numbers show (your payoff, other's payoff) under each combination of decisions.

That is, when the bank state is Low,

  • If you choose Withdraw in Period 1 (W) → you will receive {{C.ENDOWMENT}}, regardless of the other's choice.
  • If you choose Stay in Period 1 and Withdraw in Period 2 (SW) → you will receive {{sw_payoff}}, regardless of the other's choice.
  • If you choose Stay in Period 1 and Stay in Period 2 (SS) → you will receive {{C.FAIL_PAYOFF}}, regardless of the other's choice.
